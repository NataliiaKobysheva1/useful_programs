# Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint

## The plot and main characters

### Introduction

**Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint** is a popular South Korean apocalyptic fantasy web novel and *Webtoon* created by the aiuthor duo ***singNsong.***

### **Main characters**

- **Kim Dokja**   

  - The main protagonist of Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint. He is the sole reader of the webnovel Three Ways to Survive in a Ruined World, an apocalyptic fantasy story written by tls123, who read it through the end. ~~And also the author of this.~~

- **Yoo Joonghyuk** 

  - The main character of tls123's novel 'Three Ways to Survive in a Ruined World'. Throughout Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint, he is mainly seen in his 3rd and 1863rd regressions.
 
- **Han Sooyoung** 

  - One of Kim Dokja's trusted allies and a member of <Kim Dokja's Company>. She is an author and wrote the novel "SSSSS-grade Infinite Regressor" .
